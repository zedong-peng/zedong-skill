---
name: zedong-dream-big
description: The user's habit of stepping back before judging a direction. Use when choosing or assessing a direction, plan or approach, in research, engineering or anything else, before work on it starts.
---

Before judging a direction or plan, step back. Does the problem itself have taste? Is the goal big enough? Is there a bigger solution that makes the problem disappear?

If it isn't worth doing, say so plainly and recommend not doing it.

---
name: zedong-daily
description: What the user wants in the morning briefing. Use when writing their daily report of mail and news.
---

One message each morning, in Chinese, mail first, then news. Say nothing when nothing matters.

Mail: only what needs the user to read or reply: people who matter, deadlines, bills, security notices. Read, never reply.

News, at most six items, strongest first, each a link and one sentence on why it matters:

- First, training and algorithms with real signal, LLM or not: pretraining, post-training, RL, optimizers, data, architectures, scaling, open training runs.
- Then anything truly major in any field that a researcher would notice the same day.

arXiv is mostly noise: cite a paper only once it has independent signal, such as a lab post, a substantive researcher thread, strong discussion, or released code or training runs. Skip launches, takes, marketing and repeats.

Match the raw HTML and CSS of https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2. Download the page and its stylesheets; a rendered or markdown summary loses the design. Colors and fonts follow the brand-guidelines skill.

A page tells one story with as little text as it needs. For a survey: state the question, lay out related work in one table that describes each entry neutrally, then show where things stand in a figure whose caption states the headline in plain words.

Sources are inline links. Notes are unlinked superscripts after the punctuation, collected under Footnotes at the end of the page.

A public page carries sourced facts and ideas the user has approved, never Claude's own untested proposals or tips.

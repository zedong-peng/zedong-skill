---
name: zedong-html-blog
description: The user's style for a standalone HTML blog or survey page. Use when creating or revising one.
---

The page takes the theme of zedongpeng.com and sets no fonts or colors of its own. Start from https://zedongpeng.com/assets/template/page.html, which links the site's stylesheet and loads its header.

The theme is whatever an ordinary anthropic.com article actually does. Measure the rendered page, its computed fonts, sizes and colors; do not recall it or read it off a summary. Where Anthropic's typefaces are proprietary, use the open ones its brand-guidelines skill names. Keep it as spare as the reference: few sizes, few colors.

A page tells one story with as little text as it needs. For a survey: state the question, lay out related work in one table that describes each entry neutrally, then show where things stand in a figure.

Sources are inline links. Notes are unlinked superscripts after the punctuation, collected under Footnotes at the end of the page.

A survey collects and presents: data and descriptions, without analysis or commentary of its own. A public page carries sourced facts and ideas the user has approved, never Claude's own untested proposals or tips.
